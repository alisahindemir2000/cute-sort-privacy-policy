01 · Information We Collect

Cute Sort has no account or sign-in. It does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts, and it does not access your camera, microphone, photos, or precise (GPS) location.

The game does use the Google services described in Sections 3 and 4, which automatically collect technical and usage data such as device identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you play. This data is not linked to your name or email address.

02 · Information Stored On Your Device

Cute Sort saves your current level, remaining hints, and the exact arrangement of beads locally on your device, so the game can resume where you left off.

This saved game stays on your device and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

03 · Advertising & Google AdMob

Cute Sort shows ads between levels, and optional video ads to skip a level, using Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising service. To choose, deliver, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and its partner networks may automatically collect information such as your advertising identifier, device type and settings, general location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. We only see aggregated ad performance reports.

04 · Analytics, Crash Reports & Google Firebase

Cute Sort uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played, to find and fix crashes, and to adjust game settings without an app update. Firebase is provided by Google, which processes this data on our behalf.

Google Analytics for Firebase

  • Gameplay events: when a level starts, is completed, or is skipped, the level number, how many hints were used, and how long the level took.
  • App usage: app opens, sessions, screen views, engagement time, and ad impressions.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and country/region derived from your IP address.
  • Identifiers: a random app-instance ID created by Firebase, and your device's advertising identifier.

Firebase Crashlytics

  • If the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent. It contains technical details of the error, device and operating system information, the level you were on, and a random installation ID used to count how many players are affected.

Firebase Remote Config

  • The game downloads settings (for example, how many hints each level gives, or how often ads appear). To do this it sends basic technical information such as app version, operating system version, language, country, time zone, and a random installation ID.

We use this data only to improve the game, balance level difficulty, fix bugs, and measure ad performance. We see it in aggregated reports and do not use it to identify you personally.

05 · Children's Privacy

Cute Sort is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has used the game and you would like their data removed, contact us at the address in Section 11 and we will help.

06 · Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Data is shared only with:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners, to show and measure ads (Section 3);
  • Google Firebase, which processes analytics, crash, and configuration data on our behalf (Section 4);
  • authorities, if required by law.

07 · Data Retention

  • Saved game: kept on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for the retention period configured for the game, at most 14 months, and then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google according to Google's own retention policies.

08 · Your Choices

  • Reset your progress — uninstalling Cute Sort removes all locally saved data with it. Reinstalling creates new random app-instance and installation IDs.
  • Manage ad personalization — you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) or adjust ad personalization at Google's Ads Settings .
  • Request deletion — you can ask us to delete analytics data related to your device by writing to the address in Section 11. Because we do not know your name or email address, we may ask for details that help us find the data (for example, the date and device you played on).

09 · Security

We do not run our own servers. Data sent to Google AdMob and Google Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Google's infrastructure. Your saved game stays local to your device, protected by your device's own security.
