01 · Information We Collect

Cute Games does not collect any personal information through Cute Sort. The game has no account or sign-in, does not ask for your name, email address, or contacts, and does not access your camera, microphone, or location.

02 · Information Stored On Your Device

Cute Sort saves your current level and the exact arrangement of beads locally on your device, so the game can resume where you left off.

This information stays on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

03 · Advertising & Google AdMob

Cute Sort shows ads between levels using Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising service. To choose and deliver ads, AdMob and its partner networks may automatically collect technical information such as your advertising identifier, device type, general location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

We do not receive, see, or store this information ourselves — it is collected and processed directly by Google, under Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Partner Sites & Apps:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

04 · Children's Privacy

Cute Sort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age — as explained in Section 1, we don't collect personal information at all.

If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us at the address in Section 9 and we will look into it.

05 · Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because we do not collect any. The only outside party involved in Cute Sort is Google AdMob, described in Section 3, which operates under its own privacy policy.

06 · Your Choices

  • Reset your progress — uninstalling Cute Sort removes all locally saved data with it.
  • Manage ad personalization — adjust this any time from your device's ad settings, or at Google's Ads Settings .

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete — but you're always welcome to write to us with a question.

07 · Security

Cute Sort does not transmit or store personal data on any server we operate — we don't run one. Your saved progress stays local to your device, protected by your device's own security.

08 · Changes to This Policy

If Cute Sort's features change in a way that affects this policy — for example, adding a new service — we will update this page and its effective date above.

09 · Contact Us

Questions about this policy or Cute Sort? Reach Cute Games at alisahindemir76@gmail.com .
